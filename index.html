Simulation automation

SI/PI analysis that keeps pace with product development

Signal integrity and power integrity simulation is how hardware teams find out whether a design will work before anyone builds it. On a product schedule, that analysis has to happen every time a stackup, a via pattern, or a component placement changes — not once, at the end.

In practice it's the setup that stops that from happening: assembling stackups, configuring solvers, sweeping corners, collecting eye diagrams and impedance plots into a report someone can actually act on. That work is manual, repetitive, and easy to get subtly wrong under deadline pressure — which is exactly when a design most needs it done right.

Wildbrook automates that setup and reporting, so re-running SI/PI analysis costs minutes instead of a day, and engineers spend their time interpreting margin and closing timing — not re-assembling the same simulation for the fifth revision of a board.

ClariPage

Turning handwritten notes into study material

ClariPage is our app for students. Photograph a page of handwritten class notes and it produces organized study notes, explanatory diagrams, and flashcards — with every point labelled by where it came from: what the student wrote, what came off a printed handout, and what had to be added because the notes left it out.

It is built for families, with the parent as the account holder. Notes are written at the child's reading level and can be read aloud. Photographs are processed and discarded rather than stored. Our privacy policy sets out exactly what is collected and what is not.
