Software that does the tedious half of engineering work.

Wildbrook is a software company in Cedar Park, Texas. We build tools that take slow, repetitive technical work and run it automatically — in hardware simulation, and in how students study.

Simulation automation

Signal and power integrity, without the setup

Signal integrity and power integrity simulation is how hardware teams find out whether a design will actually work before anyone builds it. The analysis is valuable. The work of setting it up — assembling stackups, configuring solvers, sweeping parameters, collecting results — is slow, manual, and easy to get subtly wrong.

Wildbrook builds software that automates that setup and the reporting around it, so engineers spend their time interpreting results rather than producing them.

ClariPage

Turning handwritten notes into study material

ClariPage is our app for students. Photograph a page of handwritten class notes and it produces organized study notes, explanatory diagrams, and flashcards — with every point traceable back to the page it came from.

It is built for families, with the parent as the account holder. Photographs are processed and discarded rather than stored. Our privacy policy sets out exactly what is collected and what is not.

ClariPage is in testing and not yet publicly available.
