Is this cheating?

The question parents ask first

Will ClariPage do my child's homework?

No. It works on notes and handouts, not assignments. It organizes what was taught and names what's missing. Point it at a worksheet expecting answers and you'll be disappointed, which is the intended outcome.

How is this different from a chatbot?

A chatbot gives your child a wall of confident text with no way to tell what came from the teacher and what the machine made up. ClariPage labels every point with where it came from. You can always see what was added.

Will my child's teacher have a problem with it?

Show them. ClariPage doesn't produce anything to hand in. It produces study material from the teacher's own lesson, and it flags what your child failed to write down. Most teachers care more about that second part.

Won't my child stop taking notes if an app will fix them?

Fair worry. It's why every set of notes ends by naming what was missed. We want a student who takes better notes next semester, not one who stops trying. If ClariPage ever makes note-taking feel pointless, we built it wrong.

Will it work for us?

Before you spend an evening on it

What grades is it for?

Elementary school through high school. Set the reading level and the notes, questions and diagrams all follow it. It does the most good in the years when note-taking is still being learned, but it works just as well for a junior revising chemistry.

My child's handwriting is terrible. Will it cope?

Usually. It's built for real student handwriting, not neat samples. And it tells you when it struggled: every set carries a read-confidence rating, so a hard page is marked rather than quietly guessed at.

What subjects work best?

Anything taught from notes or a handout. It's strongest on subjects with structure, like math, science, geography and history. Vocabulary and definitions work well in any subject. Pages that are mostly long prose produce fewer useful flashcards, because there's less to test.

What if it's a typed handout instead of handwriting?

That's common and fully supported. Those points get their own label, so you can still tell what your child wrote from what the teacher gave them.

What if it gets something wrong?

Sometimes it will. Handwriting gets misread, and a diagram is occasionally off. That's why the confidence rating and the labels exist. A student who checks is better off than one who trusts.

Does it replace a tutor?

No. A tutor teaches. ClariPage tells you what to teach, or what to ask the teacher for. If anything it makes a tutoring hour more useful, because you arrive knowing which concept went missing.

Using it day to day

The practical details

Do I have to type anything in?

No. You photograph the page and everything else comes from it: notes, diagrams, flashcards, test questions. If you've ever built a flashcard set by hand at ten at night, this is the part that gives you the evening back.

How long does it take?

About a minute for a few pages. You don't have to wait on the screen. It keeps working if you go do something else and tells you when it's ready.

Can my child use it alone?

That's the goal, especially for younger kids. Notes are written at their reading level and everything can be read aloud, so a second grader can work through flashcards without an adult beside them.

Can two kids share one account?

Yes, but their study sets share one library. There are no separate per-child profiles yet. If you'd rather keep them apart, use one account each. Per-child profiles are on our list.

Does it work without a signal?

Studying does. Flashcards, practice and the review queue all work offline, because everything lives on the device. Photographing new pages needs a connection.

What devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad during testing. Android isn't built yet.

Is there a limit on how much we can use it?

Photographing pages has a monthly cap, because processing an image costs us money. Studying has none and never will.

What does it cost?

Nothing during testing. We haven't set pricing yet. Whatever we land on, the free allowance will be enough to be genuinely useful and studying will stay unlimited.

Privacy and safety

What happens to your child's work

Do you keep the photos?

No. Images are sent for processing and deleted once the notes come back. They're not stored in any database or file store of ours.

Does my child need their own account?

No, and they can't have one. A parent, guardian or teacher holds the account. Kids study on it and never enter their own details.

Is my child's work used to train AI models?

No. We don't enable any option that would allow it, and we don't sell or share your information for marketing.

Are there ads or tracking?

None. No advertising, no analytics trackers, no profiling, nothing sold. There's no advertising identifier in the app.

What if someone photographs something that isn't schoolwork?

It's refused. Nothing is read, transcribed or generated from it. That check runs before anything else happens.

Can I delete everything?

Yes. Delete an individual set in the app, or email us to delete the account and everything in it. Our privacy policy says exactly what's kept, for how long, and how to remove it.
